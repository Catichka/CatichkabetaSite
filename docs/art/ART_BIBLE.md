# CatichkaBeta website: Art Bible

ChatGPT owns art direction and asset production. Claude owns the site's code and integrates the art. Cat reviews and approves.

**Source of truth:** `docs/art/approved-handoff/START-HERE.md` and its references. `references/01-approved-full-page.png` is the approved overall composition. If anything here disagrees with it, the handoff wins.

## In short
- Friendly, playful, slightly nerdy soft cyberpunk: near-black, flat 2D pastel contour illustrations, gentle glow, lots of empty space. Organic circuit sprouts link nature and technology. Keep decoration restrained.
- Logo: the druid, Cat's old university comic character, as a simple lavender monoline (tall rounded hood, empty face opening, broad drooping sleeves, rounded hem). No cat, eyes, staff or extra details.
- One clean, friendly sans-serif (the site uses Outfit).

## Mandatory first line for every image prompt
> Playful soft-cyberpunk illustration, clean shapes, pastel neon glow (lavender, mint, peach, baby blue, pink) on a near-black background, subtle grid and wireframe details, friendly and a little nerdy, not gritty, no text unless asked.

For the druid, keep the stricter flat, simple monoline treatment.

## Technical rules
- PNG at the largest size possible, transparent background, no white halos (everything sits on near-black).
- No text baked into illustrations.
- Lowercase, hyphenated file names.
